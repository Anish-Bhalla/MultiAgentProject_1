# DEMO DATA ONLY — Quarterly Business Update

**Company:** Northstar Outdoor Supply (fictional company for software testing)

**Period:** Q2 FY2025 (April–June 2025)

**Publication date:** 15 July 2025

> This document is synthetic demonstration content. It is not a real company's filing or an investment recommendation.

## Executive summary

Net sales increased 8.4% year over year to ₹148 crore. Growth was supported by wholesale demand for hiking footwear and direct-to-consumer sales of lightweight daypacks. The wholesale channel grew 11%, while direct-to-consumer sales grew 6%.

## Demand and product mix

Management observed stronger demand for hiking footwear and insulated drinkware. Sales of rain jackets were below plan because rainfall started later than expected in two important regions. The company believes the footwear launch contributed to higher average order value, but notes that promotional activity also supported unit volumes.

## Profitability

Gross margin decreased from 42.1% to 40.7% year over year. Management attributed most of the decline to inbound freight costs, a higher share of discounted products, and promotional spending around the footwear launch. Management stated that freight rates had begun to stabilize in June, but did not provide a guarantee that margin would recover in the next quarter.

## Inventory and operations

Inventory at quarter end was ₹39 crore, up 14% year over year. Management attributed the increase to planned stocking before the autumn selling period and slower-than-planned rain-jacket sell-through. The company said it would monitor inventory ageing and reduce replenishment for slow-moving styles.

## Forward-looking commentary

Management expects wholesale orders for the autumn season to be higher than the previous year, based on distributor indications received before the publication date. The statement is an expectation, not a confirmed order book. No full-year earnings forecast was provided. Capacity at the footwear supplier is expected to increase by approximately 10% in the second half, subject to supplier execution.

## Risks

The company identified freight costs, discounting, demand uncertainty, inventory ageing, supplier delivery delays, and foreign-exchange movement as risks. The update does not quantify the expected financial impact of these risks.
